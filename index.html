About

For the past year I worked at Tessell, a database-as-a-service company, on the part of the platform that changes customers' production databases: patching them, failing them over, resizing their storage. Every change had the same rule: it must not break anything.

Most of my work was in Java and Spring Boot, with some Go, on PostgreSQL and AWS. I like the unglamorous parts: careful migrations, validation that catches the bad request before it reaches a database, and test suites that prove two systems really behave the same.

Before that I built a digital-currency wallet on an NFC smartcard as an intern, and I still spend evenings on competitive programming.
